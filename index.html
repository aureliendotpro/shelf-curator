Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 28, 2026

Short version: Shelf Curator has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your shelves stay on your iPhone. The only things that leave it are the book searches you make (sent to public book databases) and your purchases (handled by Apple).

Data stored on your device

Shelf Curator works without an account or sign-up. Your shelves, books, row names, decorations, the photos you add (cover, spine and frame photos), the optional first name you enter during the intro, and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its Home Screen widget. The publisher has no access to them. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete all my data in the app, removes them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a book or scan a barcode. Images are processed on your device (Apple's Vision and VisionKit frameworks) to find edges, text and barcodes, and are never uploaded by the app. Photos from your library are read only through Apple's photo picker, one at a time, when you pick them.

Book search and covers

To find titles, authors and covers, the app sends what you search for (an ISBN, a title or an author, or text read from a spine) over an encrypted connection to Open Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books, and downloads covers from Open Library. Like any website, these services receive your request and your IP address; their own privacy policies apply. The publisher does not receive or store these requests.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions, the free trial and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The publisher never receives your name, email or payment details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription or purchase. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder on your device two days before the trial ends. It is created on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing your shelf

The share picture is drawn on your device. It only goes where you send it, through the iOS share sheet; the publisher doesn't receive it.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking code. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. No personal data is sold, rented or shared.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data. Because the publisher doesn't collect or store personal data, everything is already under your control on your device. For any question or request, write to breakbits.apps@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Common questions

How do I add books? Open a shelf, tap +, then search by title or author, scan the barcode on the back, or photograph the cover or spine.

How do I move, resize or delete something? Drag it to move it. Hold it without moving for options: flip a book between spine and cover, make it bigger or smaller, delete it. Double-tap also deletes. Hold a row's name to resize or rename it.

How do I add the widget? Design one in the app's Widgets tab, then touch and hold your Home Screen › Edit › Add Widget › Shelf Curator, and pick the size you designed. Touch and hold the widget › Edit Widget to choose a design.

How do I restore my purchase? In the app, Settings (gear icon on the Shelves tab) › Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Shelf Curator, or Settings › Manage subscription in the app.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
