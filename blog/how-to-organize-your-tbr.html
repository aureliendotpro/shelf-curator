How to organize your TBR pile: 10 shelf ideas that work

A to-be-read pile is a promise to your future self. Organized well, it becomes the first place you look for your next book instead of a quiet source of guilt.

By the Shelf Curator team · Updated November 11, 2026

Every reader knows the feeling: the pile grows faster than you read, and the more it grows, the harder it is to choose. The fix isn't reading faster. It's making your TBR easy to browse, so the right book jumps out when you're ready for it. Here are ten ways to sort it, on a real shelf or in an app.

1. "Up next": a short shelf of 3 to 5 books

The single most useful shelf. Keep only the few books you genuinely want to read next. When you finish one, promote another. A short list beats a mountain.

2. By mood

"Cozy", "Heartbreak", "Can't put it down", "Make me think". You rarely choose a book by genre; you choose it by how you want to feel tonight.

3. By season

Summer reads, spooky October, a winter shelf of thick novels for long evenings. Seasonal shelves make the pile feel like it's rotating, even when it isn't.

4. By length

"Under 250 pages" is a lifesaver for reading slumps and busy weeks. Short books rebuild momentum.

5. Book club and buddy reads

A shelf for the next club picks, with the meeting date in mind. No more realising the night before that you haven't started.

6. Gifted and borrowed

Books from friends deserve a shelf of their own, partly out of love and partly because borrowed books should go home one day.

7. Series in order

Line up the next books in each series you're reading, so book two is waiting when book one ends.

8. "Been here too long"

Every pile has books bought years ago. Give them a shelf and one honest question each: still excited? If not, it's okay to let them go.

9. Rainbow

Sorting by spine colour is a bit of a meme, but it works for visual memory: plenty of readers remember a book's cover long before its title.

10. Favourites, for inspiration

Keep a shelf of all-time favourites next to your TBR. When you can't decide, it reminds you what you love, and what to look for next.

Make your TBR something you want to look at
