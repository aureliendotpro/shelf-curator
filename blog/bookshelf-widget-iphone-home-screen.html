How to put a bookshelf on your iPhone Home Screen

A Home Screen widget turns your TBR from something you forget into something you see fifty times a day. Here's how to set one up in a couple of minutes.

By the Shelf Curator team · Updated November 11, 2026

Why a bookshelf widget?

The books we read are usually the ones we see. A bookshelf on your Home Screen keeps your "Up next" pile in view every time you pick up your phone, and it looks a lot cozier than another row of icons. It's also become a favourite for aesthetic Home Screen setups.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
  • Shelf Curator, which draws your real shelf, with your books' covers, as a widget.

Step 1: design your widget shelf

  1. Open Shelf Curator and go to the Widgets tab.
  2. Create a widget design and pick its size: small, medium or large.
  3. Add the books you want to see: your "Up next" pile, a favourites shelf, or this month's book club pick. Arrange them, add a little decoration, and choose a theme and wood finish.

Step 2: add it to your Home Screen

  1. Go to your Home Screen and touch and hold an empty area until the icons jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Shelf Curator, pick the same size as your design, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Touch and hold the new widget, choose Edit Widget, and select your design.

Ideas for your widget

  • Small: the one book you're reading right now, cover out.
  • Medium: a two-row "Up next" shelf, perfect under your top row of apps.
  • Large: a full mini bookcase with named shelves and decorations, the centrepiece of a cozy Home Screen.
  • Match the theme to the season: a Christmas shelf in December, Halloween in October, pastel in spring.

Keep it fresh

When you finish a book, move it off your widget shelf in the app and promote the next one: your Home Screen updates on its own. For more ways to sort your pile, see how to organize your TBR.
